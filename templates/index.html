End-to-End Cryptographic & Consensus Pipeline

1

AES-256-GCM

Unique 256-bit DEK per record. 96-bit nonce and 128-bit authentication tag protect confidentiality.

2

IPFS Storage

Encrypted package stored off-chain. Content addressed via cryptographically stable CID.

3

Algorand Smart Contract

Metadata anchor (CID + SHA-256 digest) and patient consent states recorded on-chain.

4

Integrity Verification

Provider retrieval verifies on-chain digest before protected key service unwraps DEK.
